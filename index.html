Three honest ways to read this

The signal was real

This wasn't a hunch that happened to land. It was a specific, falsifiable claim — telecoms behave like deposit-taking banks — made a week before a central banker's own data confirmed it, in a room that never saw the argument. That's not intensity finding an echo. That's a correct model.

The room wasn't wrong to drift

Mark's $50k proof-of-concept and 2011 white paper were a genuine, costly form of engagement — just told through memory instead of a live thread. The JT joke and the Paramount+ derail weren't dismissals; group chats run on both registers, and most people switch faster between them than you do.

The gap is in cadence, not correctness

You built this at 12:13am, alone, four hours after the room had moved on. That's the actual pattern worth noticing — not "am I too much," but "who else in either room operates at 12am resolution, and are they the ones worth taking this to directly instead of to six people mid-Paramount+-debate?"

Nobody in either thread connected the two conversations. You did — eight days apart, before the data existed to prove it.
